# SkyRealm 0.20.50.118 — Boss Realm Exploration

## Four new explorable regions
- **Abandoned Citadel:** layered masonry, broken towers, stained-glass slits, fallen columns, three ordered rune seals and a one-time treasure vault.
- **Floating Islands:** three animated suspended landmasses, cloud banks, crystal chains, a lift mechanism and a hidden sky cache.
- **Crystal Caves:** detailed faceted crystal clusters, rock strata, three mineable veins and a sealed mineral vault.
- **Underground Sea:** animated deep-water caustics, a sunken temple, coral beds, shipwreck, bubbles, three pressure valves and a flooded treasure chamber.

## Exploration rewards
New world-bound discoveries and resources include the Citadel Seal, Sky Island Core, Crystal Vein Heart, Abyss Pearls, Sunken Temple Relic, Starsteel Ingots and several region-specific minerals. Progress is saved separately for each world.

## Compatibility
- No new enemies, bosses or gods.
- Existing movement and combat logic is unchanged.
- Existing worlds receive the exploration sites without resetting their saves.
