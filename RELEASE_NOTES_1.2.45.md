# SkyRealm Launcher 1.2.45 — Prime battle overhaul

- Reworked Prime terrain rendering with finer deterministic ground detail and smooth canvas scaling.
- Rebuilt player, ally, bot and soldier rendering with layered armor, faces, eyes, shadows and animated limbs.
- Bots patrol their bases, detect intruders, respond to attacks, and start with two defensive towers.
- Added perimeter walls to player and bot bases.
- Player respawns immediately after defeat; the match ends only when a team's towers are destroyed.
- Simplified the battle toolbar to construction only and moved the live match balance to the top center.
- Removed bot-development/attack status callouts and the arena-size overlay.
- Added F11 fullscreen toggle and expanded reversible Russian/English UI translations.
