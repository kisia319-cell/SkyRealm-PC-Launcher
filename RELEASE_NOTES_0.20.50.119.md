# SkyRealm 0.20.50.119 — Boss Realm Hub

## Dimensional Forge
- Detailed forge hall with animated fire core, basalt masonry, copper trim, anvil, chains and three recipe stations.
- Craft the Abyss Blade and Tide Trident from resources discovered in the four new regions.
- Perform artifact evolution at the forge using the existing artifact progression system and rare regional materials.

## Library of Eternity
- Four illustrated lore volumes, each with its own unlock condition and reward.
- Read the history of the Citadel, Sky Islands, Crystal Caves and Underground Sea.
- A route atlas can be compiled and saved to the world.

## Explorer Sanctuary
- Detailed stone sanctuary with carved pillars, living vines, a healing crystal and drifting light motes.
- Restore health to 100 once every three minutes.
- Register four regional relics in the trophy museum; completing the collection grants a bonus cache.
- Discoveries, recipes, lore and sanctuary progress persist per world.

## Compatibility
- No new mobs, bosses or gods.
- Existing movement logic is untouched.
- Existing saves are retained.
