# 0.20.50.180 — Three secret apocalypse laboratories

- Added exactly three hidden, hard-to-find laboratory complexes across the Apocalypse map. They are not marked on the map.
- Each complex has a detailed exterior, a discoverable entrance, four explorable floors, damaged fencing, warning lights, generators and environmental props.
- Floor 1 contains a large archive/library with rows of bookcases, cabinets, desks, research notes and searchable storage.
- Floor 2 is a quarantine wing with containment cells, barred glass, infected test subjects and escaped zombies.
- Floor 3 contains biosynthesis labs, specimen tanks, medical workstations and research storage.
- Floor 4 contains the command center, reactor equipment and a powerful mutated infected.
- Added zombie movement, contact damage, weapon combat, persistent defeated enemies and searchable loot.
- Laboratory progress is saved locally. The normal-world edition and player walking/input handlers are not changed.
