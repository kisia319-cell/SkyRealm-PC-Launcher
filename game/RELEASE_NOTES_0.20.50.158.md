# SkyRealm Game 0.20.50.158

## Adventure Guild
- Added a physical announcement board beside the guild hall and animated residents with in-game daily schedules: rest, work, socialize and patrol.
- Added four buildable and upgradeable guild rooms: Archive, Trophy Hall, Dormitory and Workshop. Their bonuses affect contract rewards, expedition damage taken and expedition materials. Room levels and relic displays are saved separately in each world.

## Guild Mail — 30-day story
- Replaced the repeating short mail cycle with a 30-day connected campaign about a missing caravan, an ancient tower, a threatened village and the Ash Seal.
- Four player decisions are saved per world and alter later letters. The final letter rewards reflect the decisions made. Daily tasks remain available and progress is retained.

## Puzzle dungeons
- Rooms 1–4 now contain rune-order puzzles with distinct sequences and visible symbol clues. Players must activate the three runes in the correct order before the exit door unlocks.
- Puzzle progress and solved rooms are saved with the world.
- Improved dungeon visuals with layered stonework, animated torch flames, rune platforms, progress indicators and lighting.

## Compatibility
- Existing guild, mail and world saves remain readable.
- Player walking, WASD controls and movement physics were not modified.
