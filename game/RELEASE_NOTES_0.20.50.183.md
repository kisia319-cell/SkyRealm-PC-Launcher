# SkyRealm 0.20.50.183

## Expanded starting area
- Replaced the empty starting surroundings with a detailed settlement hub in the normal world and a survivor checkpoint in Apocalypse.
- Added a command shelter, field medical point, quarantine tent, workshop, supply depot, watchtower, radio mast, generator, evacuation vehicle, sandbags, barriers, lanterns, crates, barrels, paths and animated survivors in Apocalypse.
- Added a starter village with a village elder house, workshop, guest shelter, market, storehouse, well, garden beds, lanterns, paths and villagers in the normal world.
- Cleared procedural tree/rock clutter from the spawn hub so the new plaza and paths remain readable.
- The settlement is deterministic and visual-only: player movement, joystick, collision logic, world saves and progression are not changed.
