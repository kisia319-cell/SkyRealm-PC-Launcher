# SkyRealm 0.20.50.150 — Adventure Guild Expansion

## Six new gameplay systems
- **Adventure Guild:** four rotating daily contracts for hunting, mining, forestry, construction, fishing and exploration, with gold, guild experience and festival tokens.
- **Expeditions:** three routes (Forgotten Ruins, Frost Pass and Abyss Rift) with 2–3 waves of enemies, weapon-based attacks, incoming damage and escalating rewards.
- **Treasure maps:** spend 35 gold to mark a random treasure location in the current world; approach within 95 world units to claim the cache.
- **Camp development:** upgrade the Hearth, Workshop, Observatory and Infirmary up to level III for persistent world bonuses.
- **Relic collection:** six collectible relics; each found relic improves expedition damage and contract gold rewards.
- **Season festival:** exchange earned tokens for potions, crystals and mithril.

## Five polish passes
- Per-world persistent guild progress with validated state loading.
- Daily contract rotation keyed to in-game day, with old expedition enemies cleaned up on rollover.
- Reward and combat bonuses scale with camp upgrades and collected relics.
- Co-op and special interior/dungeon modes are guarded from unsupported guild actions.
- Responsive guild interface, explicit progress feedback and autosave on visibility change / exit.

Player movement and walking code were not modified.
