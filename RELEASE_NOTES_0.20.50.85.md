# SkyRealm 0.20.50.85 — House access, companions and audio settings

- Fixed the blocked house doorway by removing collision from a decorative basket in the exit path.
- Workbench interaction now uses a wider radius around the work surface, allowing access from every reachable side.
- Rebuilt all six companion visuals: fox, wolf, owl, turtle, phoenix and dragon now have distinct anatomy, face details, animated movement, markings, glows and particles.
- Added an in-game Audio settings tab with background music toggle, music volume, effects volume and interface sound toggle. Settings are saved and applied immediately.
- Improved staff-role refresh on returning to the main menu and shortened retries when the server has not yet confirmed a role.
- Player movement and walking calculations were not changed.
- Automated checks and full in-game visual playtesting are still required.
